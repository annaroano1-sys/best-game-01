STARFALL

Collect the stars. Dodge the rocks. Sink the mothership.

Campaign: 15 missions across 3 sectors — beat the mothership on level 15.
Endless Drift: survive as long as you can. Difficulty never stops climbing.

HOW TO PLAY

Move with Arrow keys / WASD or the mouse. Hold Space or the mouse button to fire.

Missions: every level has one goal — collect stars, destroy enemies, survive, or sink the boss. Finish it to earn stars ★ and shards ◆, then spend shards in the shop.

Combo: collect stars quickly to raise the multiplier. Graze a rock for +5.

Power-ups: S shield · ◷ slow-mo · ♥ extra life · M magnet · ×2 double score · ⁂ spread fire · ✹ screen bomb.

Enemies: rocks, speeders, splitters, mines (keep your distance — they explode), drones, gunners that shoot back, swarms, and colossi. A raider gunship guards every 3rd level; a mothership guards every 5th.

P pause · M mute · R restart after game over.
