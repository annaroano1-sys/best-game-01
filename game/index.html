STARFALL

Collect the stars. Dodge the rocks. Sink the mothership.

Campaign: 15 missions across 3 sectors — beat the mothership on level 15.
Endless Drift: survive as long as you can. Difficulty never stops climbing.
Boss Rush: all three sector bosses back to back, with a shop breather between them.

HOW TO PLAY

1 · Fly. Move with Arrow keys / WASD or steer with the mouse. Hold Space or the mouse button to fire — big rocks take two hits, colossi take six.

2 · Collect. Catch golden stars: each one scores and feeds your combo — grab the next one quickly to keep the chain alive. Rare golden comets pay 50. Graze a rock without touching it for +5.

3 · Finish the mission. Every level has one goal — collect stars, destroy enemies, survive 45 seconds, or sink the boss. Finish it clean (no hits) for ★★★ and a shard reward.

4 · Spend shards in the shop. Between levels, buy rapid fire, heavy rounds, spread cannon, hull, shield and magnet upgrades — each with 3 ranks.

5 · Sink the guardians. A raider gunship hunts you every 3rd level; a mothership guards every 5th. Each sector's boss fights differently — the Ember Hive breathes fire, the Void Wraith blinks across the sky.

💣 Bombs. Every run starts with 3 bombs — press B or tap the big 💣 button (bottom right) to clear the sky: every enemy on screen takes a huge hit, all enemy bullets vanish, and guardians take heavy damage (but never die instantly). Finish a mission to earn +1 bomb, up to 5. Save them for the boss.

Power-ups: S shield · ◷ slow-mo · ♥ extra life · M magnet · ×2 double score · ⁂ spread fire · ✹ screen bomb · R overdrive · G ghost (fly through anything) · ⇶ piercing shots (bullets fly through enemies).

Enemies: rocks, speeders, splitters, mines (keep your distance — they explode), drones, gunners that shoot back, swarms, colossi, dashers that charge at you, snipers that draw an aim line before firing, weavers that sine-weave across the sky, and carriers — slow freighters that drop a power-up when cracked open.

Boss Rush: fight the Mothership, the Ember Hive and the Void Wraith back to back, with a shop breather between bosses. Sink all three to win.

P pause · M mute · R restart after game over.
